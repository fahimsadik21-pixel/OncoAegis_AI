5. Important Medical Rule
=========================
Imaging model থেকে সরাসরি “Cancer confirmed” বলবে না, যদি definitive pathology evidence না থাকে।

Imaging output হবে:
- suspicious lesion
- suspicious for malignancy
- high-risk appearance
- model probability
- indeterminate
- requires review

Pathology/biopsy report explicit cancer confirmation দিলে system বলতে পারবে:
“Supplied pathology report documents confirmed malignancy.”

9. Cross-Modal Evidence Verification
====================================
Example:
CT: Suspicious liver lesion
Pathology: Hepatocellular carcinoma
Clinical: AFP elevated

System:
CT Evidence + Pathology Evidence + Clinical Evidence
→ Evidence Fusion
→ Agreement Check
→ Unified Cancer Profile

Conflict:
Imaging: Suggests malignant lesion
Pathology: Benign finding

Output:
Evidence conflict detected.
Automated definitive conclusion withheld.
Expert review required.

1. PRODUCT VISION
============================================================

OncoAegis AI is an adaptive multimodal cancer detection and analysis assistant. It is not a single classifier, a chatbot-only project, or an autonomous diagnostic system.

The architecture is:

    User
      |
      v
    Professional assistant/orchestrator
      |
      +--> Imaging specialists: CT, ultrasound, MRI, histopathology
      +--> Document specialists: PDF, TXT, OCR, pathology, radiology, lab
      +--> Clinical evidence normalizer
      +--> Measurement and morphology engine
      +--> Multimodal evidence fusion
      +--> Safety and uncertainty guard
      |
      v
    Structured analysis + human-readable report + expert-review status

The assistant coordinates specialist models. A generic LLM may explain structured outputs and organize evidence, but it must not replace the medical computer-vision models or invent a cancer diagnosis.

============================================================
2. NON-NEGOTIABLE MEDICAL SAFETY
============================================================

Imaging alone must never be described as confirmed cancer. Use language such as:

- suspicious lesion
- suspicious for malignancy
- high-risk appearance
- indeterminate finding
- requires expert review

Only pathology or biopsy-confirmed information may be represented as confirmed malignancy evidence. Keep the source, date, document section, and confidence for every such statement.

If sources disagree, explicitly show the conflict. Example:

    Imaging: suspicious lesion
    Pathology: benign lesion
    Final status: evidence conflict detected; definitive conclusion withheld

Every result must expose:

- evidence and source provenance
- model and checkpoint used
- uncertainty/confidence
- limitations and missing information
- expert-review requirement
- research-only disclaimer where appropriate

Never fabricate a missing report, pathology result, patient history, measurement, or model confidence.
